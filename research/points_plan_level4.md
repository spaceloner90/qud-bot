# Points plan at level 4 (2026-11-24): what it takes to beat a giant amoeba

Unspent: **210 skill points, 1 mutation point** (no attribute point this level). Skill texts:
`skills_level4.md` (read from the game's Buy Skills screen). Nothing spent yet.

## The problem, in numbers

**Giant amoeba** (wiki + our fights): 4 slimy pseudopods, each 1d3−1 damage with penetration from its
Strength; AV 3 / DV −1 per one wiki edit (unconfirmed); HP unknown. It's slow and gives up once out of
sight. What it did to us: 4 + 7 in one turn (turn 49180), then crit 6 (dazed us) + 8 + 7 = **21 in one
exchange** (turn 50084). Freezing Ray did 4 and 5 (rank 2); after ~9–11 damage it showed "Injured".

**Us:** HP 27, **AV 2** (furs: AV 2, DV −1), DV 6. Weapon **bronze battle axe: penetration 5,
damage 1d2**; our blows do 1–3, sometimes up to 8 with multiple penetrations. **Empty slots:** head,
face, back, both arms, both pairs of feet (Multiple Legs gives forefeet and hindfeet), missile and
thrown weapon.

**Why it hurts so much:** its damage comes in many small dice, each multiplied by how many times it
penetrates our armour ("hits (x4) for 8"). With AV 2 almost every roll penetrates. Each point of AV
cuts the number of penetrations, so armour is the biggest lever against exactly this kind of enemy.
Our side is the mirror image: a 1d2 weapon does little per hit, so we need more penetrations (Cleave) or
burst damage (the ray).

## Options

### Mutation point
- **Freezing Ray 2 → 3** (now allowed; the game showed it): damage 2d3+1 → **3d3+1** (avg ~5 → ~7),
  cooldown 20, melee cooling 2d4 → 3d4. It's our only ranged and burst damage; it one-shot the vine and
  finished the spider. Against the amoeba it means ~4 rays instead of ~6 from range.
- Multiple Legs 3 (faster escape), Teleportation 2 (tighter landing): less direct.
- **Recommended: Freezing Ray 3.**

### Skill points (210)
- **Cleave (150)**: each axe hit, 75% chance to give the target −1 AV, stacking up to 2 (half our ST
  modifier, rounded up) +1 on crits. Against AV 3 that takes it to ~1, so more of our blows penetrate.
  Helps every axe fight. Then **Charging Strike (100)** later (Cleave + Charge: extra −1 AV on a charge).
- Endurance root (100) + **Shake It Off (0)**: (TO−10)% = 8% a round to shake off daze/stun, −25%
  poison damage. Small; **Calloused (200)** later gives natural AV 1.
- Acrobatics (75) + **Spry (100)**: +2 DV (fewer hits land at all) = 175.
- Shield root (100): only worth it with a shield (we have none; our left hand holds the torch).
- **Recommended: Cleave (150), keep 60** towards Endurance or Spry at the next level.

### Gear (no points: the biggest lever)
- On Red Rock stratum 3's floor (the collect sweep): **leather cap** (head), cloth overalls, cloth robe,
  quilted shawl, iron dagger, club, bronze battle axe, **slender two-handed steel long sword** (Look at
  its damage first; two-handed means no torch in hand).
- In Joppa: Tam sells **leather armor ($14.29)** and chain mail ($71.43, too dear: we have 21 drams).
  Sell the 3 books, nuggets, bronze ingot and the lover's blossom (a tinkering ingredient) first.
- Look for boots (two pairs: forefeet and hindfeet), gloves, a cloak.

### Tactics (no cost)
- Never melee it while it can act. Ray from range, back off along a known route, wait out the
  cooldown out of its sight (it gives up), ray again. Close in only once it's Badly Wounded, at full HP,
  with no bad effects on us, and with armour on.
- Always check our own effects first (terror, daze, confusion) and wait them out somewhere safe.

## Decision (2026-11-24; the user left character development to me)

- The user's point: the ray's value is the freeze, not its damage, and saving for something big may be
  better. The freeze held the amoeba about a turn at either rank, so rank 3 isn't urgent.
- **Mutation point: saved** towards a new random mutation (4 MP; 1 MP comes per level, so ~level 7).
- **Skill points: held at 210.** Candidate for the next level (~280 SP): **Single Weapon Fighting (100)
  + Weapon Expertise (150, 21 ST; we have 22)**: each melee attack has a 50% chance of a free second
  attack, about 1.5× axe damage, a bigger step than Cleave's −2 AV. Cleave stays the fallback.
- Meal Preparation is not free: buying it asks for the Cooking and Gathering root (100). Not worth it
  while town food is this cheap (see design.md, economy).
