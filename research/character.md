# Character research: Ushrorooshum at level 2 (2026-11-24)

Read from the game's own screens (skills, character sheet, abilities). Full skill texts: `skills_level2.md`; abilities: `abilities_level2.md`. Nothing has been spent yet.

## What we have

- **Mutated Human Marauder.** ST 22 (+3), AG 18 (+1), TO 18 (+1), IN 14 (−1), WI 16 (0), EG 16 (0). HP 21. XP 225, next level at 505.
- **Unspent:** 66 skill points, 1 mutation point. A new random mutation costs 4 MP; 1 MP raises an existing mutation by one level (character sheet: "[space] Raise selected statistic or mutation").
- **Mutations:**
  - **Freezing Ray (1):** 9-square ray of frost in a chosen direction, 1d3+1 damage, cooldown 20. **Our only ranged attack, never used so far** (not even against the vine).
  - **Multiple Legs (1):** the four legs.
  - **Teleportation (1):** teleport to a random spot within an uncertainty radius of 12, cooldown 100. An escape from things that follow (the centipede).
  - **Amphibious (D):** a defect. **Very likely why we're always "Wet" and the game keeps pouring our fresh water over us** ("You pour fresh water from your waterskin over yourself", on every world-map step and sometimes in town). This makes **water a core resource** for this character: 27 drams left, two empty skins.
- **Skills owned:** Axe Proficiency (+2 to hit with axes), Dismember (activated: bleed, cd 30; passive 3–6% dismember chance), Butchery (ingredients from corpses; "Butcher Corpses" is toggled on), Charge (2–3 squares + attack, +1 penetration, cd 15), Make Camp (campfire: cooking, preserving).
- **Abilities in the bar:** Sprint (2× move speed for 10 rounds, −5 DV, −10 melee to-hit, cd 100), Freezing Ray, Make Camp, Butcher Corpses, Dismember, Charge, Teleport.

## Options for the 66 skill points

**Free (0 sp), if the game lets us take them without their category** (each category's root costs points; the class gave us some subskills without the root, so we have to test). No downside if allowed:
- **Mind's Compass** (Wayfaring, 15 IN): "You travel on the world map **twice as quickly**", and get lost less. World-map steps cost us 300 turns each, so this halves the biggest travel cost directly.
- **Shake It Off** (Endurance, 0 TO): chance each round to shake off daze/stun; 25% less poison damage.
- **Hurdle** (Tactics): no DV penalty while sprinting.
- **Meal Preparation** (Cooking and Gathering, 15 IN): cook at a campfire for status effects; preserve food.
- **Staunch Wounds** (Physic): bandages stop bleeding better.

**Worth saving for:**
- **Cleave** (Axe, 150 sp, 19 ST; we have 22): 75% chance per axe hit to give the target −1 AV, stacking up to half our ST modifier (+1 on crits). More of our blows would get through armour; several "doesn't penetrate" results were armour.
- **Fasting Way** (Self-discipline, 50 sp, **17 WI: we have 16**): hunger and thirst at half rate. For an Amphibious character that burns water, this would matter a lot, but it's blocked until WI goes up (attribute points come with some levels), and the Self-discipline root is 150.
- **Swimming** (Endurance, 100): less slowdown in water; we wade a lot in these caves.
- Wayfaring root (100) or Endurance root (100) if the free subskills turn out to need them.

**Not now:** the IN-gated skills (Tinkering, Nostrums...) are out of reach with IN 14; other weapon trees would split our points.

## Options for the 1 mutation point

- **Freezing Ray → 2:** our only ranged attack. The vine episode is exactly where a ray would have been the answer (it's immobile and can be hit from a distance). We don't know yet what a level adds (probably damage and maybe range or cooldown). A mechanical question we could look up.
- **Teleportation → 2:** probably a shorter cooldown or a tighter landing area. Escape matters, but we haven't used it once yet.
- **Multiple Legs → 2:** move speed, helpful for fleeing and travel, but less urgent.
- Saving towards a random new mutation (4 MP) is a gamble.

## Proposed plan (for the user to approve)

1. Try the **free skills**: Mind's Compass first, then Shake It Off, Hurdle, Meal Preparation, Staunch Wounds. Keep whichever the game allows; they cost 0.
2. **Save the 66 sp** towards Cleave (150), revisiting at level 3.
3. **Mutation point into Freezing Ray**, after checking what a level adds.
4. Teach the fight routine to use **Freezing Ray** (enemies at range, especially immobile ones), **Teleport** (escape when hurt against something that follows), and **Charge** to open fights.
5. Treat **water** as a first-class need for this Amphibious character: watch the drams, refill at Joppa, and consider Fasting Way once WI allows.
